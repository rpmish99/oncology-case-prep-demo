SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT. Written for a demonstration, with reference to public NCI PDQ treatment summaries. It is a simplified, illustrative set of care guidelines and is not clinical guidance.

# Scope
Adults with newly diagnosed non-small cell lung cancer.

# 1. Diagnosis and biomarker testing
1.1 Confirm histology on tissue. For adenocarcinoma and other non-squamous types, order molecular testing for EGFR, ALK, ROS1, BRAF, RET, MET, KRAS, NTRK and HER2, and PD-L1 expression, before first-line treatment is chosen.
1.2 If another note disagrees with the molecular or pathology report, the report governs, and the discrepancy is flagged for correction.

# 2. Staging
2.1 Contrast CT of chest and upper abdomen and PET/CT.
2.2 Brain MRI with contrast is required for stage III and stage IV disease. If MRI cannot be done, use contrast CT.
2.3 Confirm mediastinal nodal disease by tissue sampling when it would change treatment.

# 3. Stage IV disease
3.1 Sensitizing EGFR mutation (exon 19 deletion or L858R): an EGFR-targeted agent is the first-line treatment rather than chemotherapy with immunotherapy. Osimertinib is the preferred agent.
3.2 ALK, ROS1, RET, MET exon 14 or other actionable alteration: the matching targeted agent is first line.
3.3 No actionable alteration: treatment is guided by PD-L1. PD-L1 of 50% or more: immunotherapy alone is an option. Lower PD-L1: chemotherapy with immunotherapy.
3.4 Painful or at-risk bone metastases: discuss palliative radiation and a bone-modifying agent.

# 4. Before starting a targeted agent
4.1 Baseline electrocardiogram and review of current medications for interactions.
4.2 Baseline cardiac function for agents where it is required.
4.3 Document performance status.

# 5. Earlier stages
5.1 Stage I to II: surgery is preferred when the patient can have it. Radiation is for those who cannot or will not have surgery.
5.2 Resected stage II to IIIA: adjuvant treatment is discussed, including targeted therapy when an EGFR or ALK alteration is present.
5.3 Unresectable stage III: chemoradiation.
