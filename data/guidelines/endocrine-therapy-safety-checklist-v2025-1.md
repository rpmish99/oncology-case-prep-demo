SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT. Illustrative checklist for a demonstration. Not clinical guidance.

# Before starting an aromatase inhibitor
- Confirm postmenopausal status.
- Review fracture risk and the date and result of the last DEXA scan.
- Review cardiovascular risk factors, including hypertension and lipids.
- Review joint symptoms at baseline.

# Before starting tamoxifen
- Review history of venous thromboembolism. Prior DVT or PE: involve hematology and document the risk discussion.
- Review gynecologic history and uterine symptoms.
- Review interacting medications.

# Monitoring
- Review adherence and side effects at 3 months, then every 6 months.
- Repeat bone density according to the bone health pathway.

# Documentation
- Record who reviewed the checklist and the date.
