# Genomic assay (node-negative disease)
- Recurrence score 0 to 17: endocrine therapy alone.
- Recurrence score 18 to 30: consider chemotherapy followed by endocrine therapy for all patients regardless of menopausal status.
- Recurrence score 31 or higher: chemotherapy followed by endocrine therapy.

# Endocrine therapy
- Postmenopausal: tamoxifen for 5 years, or an aromatase inhibitor.

# Bone health
- Obtain a DEXA scan before an aromatase inhibitor.
