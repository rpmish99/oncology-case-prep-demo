SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT. Written for a demonstration, with reference to public NCI PDQ treatment summaries. It is a simplified, illustrative set of care guidelines and is not clinical guidance.

# Scope
Adults with resected adenocarcinoma of the colon.

# 1. Workup
1.1 Contrast CT of chest, abdomen and pelvis and a baseline CEA before surgery.
1.2 Mismatch repair or microsatellite instability testing on every colorectal tumor.
1.3 If another note disagrees with the pathology report about stage, the pathology report governs, and the discrepancy is flagged for correction.

# 2. Stage II
2.1 Surgery is the main treatment. Adjuvant chemotherapy is generally not used outside a clinical trial.
2.2 For high-risk features (T4, fewer than 12 nodes examined, poor differentiation), discuss adjuvant chemotherapy individually.
2.3 Mismatch repair deficient stage II without high-risk features: no fluoropyrimidine alone.

# 3. Stage III
3.1 Adjuvant oxaliplatin-based chemotherapy after surgery: CAPOX or FOLFOX.
3.2 Duration depends on risk. Low risk (T1 to T3 and N1): three months of CAPOX is preferred. High risk (T4 or N2): six months.
3.3 Start within about eight weeks of surgery when the patient has recovered.

# 4. Safety checks before starting
4.1 Review baseline peripheral neuropathy. Oxaliplatin causes cumulative neuropathy. When baseline neuropathy is significant, discuss the risk, a plan for dose changes, or a regimen without oxaliplatin.
4.2 Review kidney function and current medications.
4.3 Where available, consider DPYD genotyping before a fluoropyrimidine.

# 5. Follow-up
5.1 Surveillance follows the institutional schedule. Post-operative CEA is drawn once to confirm normalization.
